Biography

Senator Robinhood "Robin" Cariño Padilla

Senator of the Republic of the Philippines

Early Life

Robinhood Cariño Padilla was born on November 23, 1969. He became one of the country's most recognized actors, admired for portraying roles that reflected courage, patriotism, and justice.

Public Service

In 2022, he was elected Senator of the Republic of the Philippines with the highest number of votes nationwide. As a legislator, he champions constitutional reforms, education, freedom of information, cultural preservation, and programs that uplift Muslim Filipinos and Indigenous Peoples.

Legislative Priorities
  • Constitutional Reform
  • Freedom of Information
  • Education and Youth Development
  • Peace and Development
  • Cultural Preservation
  • Muslim Filipino Welfare
Vision

To build a stronger and more united Philippines through inclusive governance, effective legislation, transparency, and equal opportunities for every Filipino.
