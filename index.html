Senator Robin Padilla

Biography

Robinhood Ferdinand Cariño Padilla, popularly known as Robin Padilla, is a Filipino actor, film producer, martial artist, and public servant.

He was elected Senator of the Republic of the Philippines in 2022 after receiving the highest number of votes in the national elections.

Throughout his career, he has advocated for peace, public service, cultural preservation, and programs that promote the welfare of the Filipino people.

Public Service
  • Senator of the Republic of the Philippines
