About the Senator

Learn more about Senator Robin Padilla's life, public service, and legislative journey.

Biography

Early Life

Robinhood Ferdinand Cariño Padilla was born on November 23, 1969 in Daet, Camarines Norte. Robin Padilla is the son of Casimero "Roy" Padilla Sr., a former actor, labor leader, and Governor of Camarines Norte, and actress Lolita Eva Cariño.

He studied at Siena College of Quezon City and Saint Louis University Boys' High School before pursuing a career in acting. He later earned a Bachelor's Degree in Criminology from the Philippine College of Criminology.

Entertainment Career

Robin Padilla made his film debut in 1985 and became one of the Philippines' most recognized action stars through blockbuster films such as Maging Sino Ka Man and Ang Utol Kong Hoodlum.

Throughout his career, he worked with ABS-CBN and GMA Network, earning numerous awards and recognition in the entertainment industry.

Political Career

In 2021, Robin Padilla officially announced his candidacy for the Senate under PDP–Laban.

He was elected Senator in 2022 after receiving the highest number of votes nationwide and currently serves as Chairman of the Senate Committee on Constitutional Amendments and Revision of Codes and the Committee on Cultural Communities and Muslim Affairs.

Family

Robin Padilla married Liezl Sicangco in 1996 and later married actress Mariel Rodriguez in 2010. He is a proud father of five children.

Core Belief & Vision

Guided by faith, patriotism, and genuine public service.

Core Belief

To uphold justice, strengthen national unity, preserve Filipino culture, and ensure that every Filipino has equal opportunities through effective legislation.

Vision

To build a peaceful, progressive, and inclusive Philippines where every citizen can live with dignity, security, and hope for future generations.
